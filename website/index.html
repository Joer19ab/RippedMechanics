16-Week Compliance Overview

16-Week Program Plan

Hyrox Build + 70.3 Base — May 6 to Aug 22, 2026

Phase 1

Foundation & Diagnostics

Weeks 1–4
  • Establish consistent sleep & routine
  • Z2 base building — all 4 disciplines
  • Baseline tests: 5K, FTP, swim 400m
  • 80/20 intensity split enforced strictly
  • Introduce sled & lower body strength
Target Compliance ≥90%
Phase 2

Build Capacity

Weeks 5–8
  • Progressive volume increase (+5–10%/wk)
  • Introduce threshold intervals
  • Hyrox engine intervals (SkiErg, sled)
  • Brick sessions: bike→run
  • Mid-program test: 5K, FTP retest
Target Compliance ≥85%
Phase 3

Hyrox Specificity

Weeks 9–12
  • Compromised running after station work
  • Sled push/pull loaded intervals
  • SkiErg + Wall Balls + Burpee combos
  • Race-pace 1K repeats
  • Strength maintenance (not building)
Target Compliance ≥85%
Phase 4

Sharpen & Simulate

Weeks 13–15
  • Full Hyrox simulation race effort
  • Race-day nutrition & pacing rehearsal
  • Sharpen run speed: 400m repeats
  • Protect recovery — sleep >8h nightly
  • Final FTP & run benchmark test
Target Compliance ≥80%
Phase 5

Taper & Race

Week 16
  • Volume cut by 40–50%
  • Maintain intensity — short, sharp
  • Gear & logistics check
  • Visualise full race execution
  • Race Day: Sub-75 min execution
Race Target <75 min
