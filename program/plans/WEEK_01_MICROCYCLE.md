## Global Rules
- HR is the governor on all easy and Z2 sessions. Pace is irrelevant until HR is controlled.
- Fuel all sessions over 75 min or classified as hard: 60-90g carbs/hour, 500-800ml fluids/hour.
- Log RPE, HR average, and a 1-sentence quality note after every session.
- Run readiness gate each morning before deciding session intensity.
- Sleep window non-negotiable: 20:30 lights out, 04:30 wake.

## Monday 11 May

### AM 05:30-06:50 — Hyrox Engine Intro
**Purpose:** Establish compromised running baseline. First exposure to run-SkiErg alternation.

**Warm-up 10 min**
- 5 min easy jog HR < 130
- Leg swings forward/back x10 each leg
- Hip circles x10 each direction
- 2x20m high knees, 2x20m butt kicks
- 2x20m A-skips

**Main Set — 4 rounds, 2 min easy walk/jog rest between rounds**
- RUN 800m at 10K effort (approx 4:55–5:10/km). Not a sprint. Tall posture, cadence above 170 spm, breathe rhythmically through the mouth if needed at this effort. Stay relaxed in the upper body.
- IMMEDIATELY into SKIERG 500m. Hinge from hips on the pull, hands drive through to hip level, arms float back to eye height with relaxed elbows. Stroke rate 22–26 spm. Target pace sub-2:05 per 500m. Do not rush the recovery phase — it costs you more than you gain.
- Round target: complete each round within 10 sec of your round 1 split time. Log every split.

**Cool-down 10 min**
- 5 min easy jog
- Hip flexor kneeling lunge stretch 60 sec each side
- 8 deep diaphragmatic breaths in 90/90 position

**KPI:** Round splits within 10 sec of each other. HR recovers below 145 within rest window. Form (posture and stroke) holds across all 4 rounds.

### PM 16:30-17:10 — Mobility + Breathing Reset
**Purpose:** Parasympathetic reset. Correct chest-breathing pattern. Restore hip and thoracic mobility.

**Hip Flow 7 min**
- 90/90 hip stretch: 90 sec each side, breathing through tension
- Pigeon pose: 90 sec each side
- Kneeling hip flexor lunge: 60 sec each side, posterior pelvic tilt at end range

**Thoracic Spine Flow 5 min**
- Thread the needle: 10 slow reps each side
- Cat-cow: 15 reps, full range, 3 sec each direction
- Thoracic extension over foam roller: 60 sec upper back, 60 sec mid back

**Ankle Mobility 3 min**
- Wall ankle stretch: 30 sec each side
- Banded dorsiflexion: 30 sec each side

**Trunk Stability 10 min**
- Dead bug: 3x10 reps, 3-sec lowering phase on each rep. Lower back stays pinned to floor.
- Bird dog: 3x10 each side, 3-sec hold at full extension. No hip rotation.
- Pallof press: 3x12 each side. Brace hard before pressing out.

**Diaphragmatic Breathing 8 min**
- Lie prone (crocodile breathing). Hand on lower back to feel expansion.
- Inhale 5 sec through nose: belly and lower back expand, chest stays still.
- Hold 2 sec.
- Exhale 7 sec through pursed lips.
- 4 rounds of 8 breaths. 30 sec passive rest between rounds.
- This directly corrects your chest-breathing pattern. Do this every session this week.

**KPI:** Tension clearly reduced by end. Breathing feels lower and slower. Evening HRV trend positive.

## Tuesday 12 May

### AM 05:30-06:45 — Swim Technique + Pull Focus
**Purpose:** Build technique foundation and comfort. No fitness targets this week — mechanics only.

**Warm-up**
- 200m easy, any stroke, no effort. Feel neutral buoyancy and water position.

**Drill Set — 6x50m, 20 sec rest**
- Rep 1–2: Catch-up drill. Wait until lead arm is fully extended before initiating the pull on the other side. Forces long stroke and eliminates rushing.
- Rep 3–4: Fist drill. Closed fists. Forces you to feel the forearm and not just the palm pulling through. Open fists on the 25m swim segment.
- Rep 5–6: Bilateral breathing drill. Breathe every 3 strokes. Count and make it deliberate.

**Pull Set — 8x100m pull buoy, 15–20 sec rest**
- HR below 150. This is aerobic and technical, not hard.
- Focus: early vertical forearm on entry. Fingers point to floor at the catch before pulling back. Elbow stays high. Press the chest slightly down to float hips up.
- Count stroke cycles per 25m length. Target 16–18 cycles (32–36 arm strokes). If above 20, you are slipping through the water.
- Hold or reduce stroke count across the set.

**Build Set — 4x50m, 20 sec rest**
- Rep 1: Easy, 60% effort
- Rep 2: Moderate, 70% effort
- Rep 3: Moderate-hard, 80% effort — hold mechanics, do not collapse form for speed
- Rep 4: Hard controlled, 90% — assess where stroke breaks down under effort

**Cool-down**
- 200m easy backstroke or side stroke. Full passive rest.

**KPI:** Stroke count holds or decreases across pull set. No dropped elbow under fatigue in the build set. Breathing relaxed and bilateral.

### PM 16:15-17:30 — Run Threshold Intervals
**Purpose:** Establish threshold HR at true running effort. First structured threshold session.

**Warm-up 15 min**
- 10 min easy jog building from HR 130 to 150
- A-skip 2x20m, B-skip 2x20m, butt kicks 2x20m
- 3 strides building progressively to near-threshold pace (approx 4:45/km on stride 3)

**Main Set — 4x8 min @ HR 172–178, 2 min easy jog recovery between reps**
- Rep 1: Start conservatively at 4:50/km. Let HR climb to the band naturally by min 2. Do not force it. Target HR 172–175.
- Rep 2–3: Settle into steady state. Adjust pace to hold the HR band — if HR drifts above 178, slow 5–10 sec/km. If HR stays below 172, pick up slightly. Pace is a byproduct, not the target.
- Rep 4: Accumulated fatigue will make this feel harder. Shorten stride very slightly, keep cadence above 170 spm. If HR exceeds 180, immediately slow to 5:20/km until it drops back.
- Recovery jog: HR must return below 145 within 90 sec. If it doesn't, extend rest to 3 min and note it.

**Cool-down 10 min**
- Easy jog fading to walk
- Standing quad stretch 45 sec each, calf stretch 45 sec each, kneeling hip flexor 60 sec each

**Fuel:** 30–45g carbs 30 min pre-session (e.g. rice cakes + honey).

**KPI:** All 4 reps completed within HR band 172–178. Pace SD below 10 sec/km across reps 2–4. Form intact in rep 4. Log avg HR and avg pace per rep.

## Wednesday 13 May

### PM 16:15-17:25 — Bike Z2 Aerobic
**Purpose:** Active recovery ride. Preserve legs after Thursday double. Build aerobic base at low cardiac cost.

**Session — 60–70 min @ 130–170W, cadence 88–95 rpm throughout**

**Cadence Work — 5x1 min at 100–110 rpm**
- Spread evenly through the ride (e.g. min 15, 25, 35, 45, 55)
- Power stays flat in Z2 range during cadence reps. Spin, don't grind.
- 2 min normal cadence 88–92 rpm between each.

**Breathing Rule:** Nasal breathing only the entire session. If you cannot breathe through your nose comfortably, you are going too hard. Back off immediately and hold that lower power until breathing normalises.

**Cool-down — final 10 min**
- Power below 130W, cadence 85 rpm, spin legs loose.

**KPI:** Avg HR below 145. No cardiac drift (HR not rising over equal power blocks across the ride). Avg power stays inside Z2 range.

### AM 05:30-07:00 — Hyrox Strength Endurance Circuit
**Purpose:** Hyrox station conditioning. Build movement quality under metabolic load.

**Warm-up 8 min**
- Row 500m easy, damper 3–4
- 10 hip circles each direction
- 10 bodyweight squats slow
- 10 shoulder circles forward and back
- 10 band pull-aparts

**Main Set — 4 rounds, 3 min rest between rounds. Log every round split.**

**ROW 750m**
- Damper 4–5. Drive through legs first, then lean back, then pull arms to lower ribs. Return in reverse: arms out, lean forward, then legs. Stroke rate 24–28 spm. Target pace: sub-3:15 per 500m equivalent (so sub-2:26 for 750m). Do not rush the rate — a longer, more powerful stroke beats a fast sloppy one.

**FARMER CARRY 100m**
- 40–50kg total (20–25kg each hand). Walk tall — no torso lean side to side. Pack shoulders down and back. Brace core hard. Do not switch grip if possible. If grip fails, set down, reset, and continue. Note if grip was limiting.

**SANDBAG LUNGE 25m**
- 20kg sandbag on shoulder. Upright torso throughout — do not hinge forward. Rear knee hovers 2cm from floor on each rep. Drive explosively through the front heel. Alternate which shoulder carries the bag each round.

**BURPEE BROAD JUMP x20**
- Jump forward maximum horizontal distance on each rep. Measure your round 1 jump roughly and aim to replicate it across all 20. No pausing at the top between burpees. Chest touches floor each rep.

**WALL BALL x25**
- 9kg ball, full squat depth (hip crease below knee), explosive hip drive, release at eye level, hit target. Target: 20+ unbroken in round 1. In rounds 3–4, manage breaks before failure — stop at 15, rest 5 sec, finish 10.

**KPI:** All 4 rounds completed. Round 4 split within 45 sec of round 1. Movement quality maintained (no form collapse on lunges or squat depth on wall balls). All round splits logged.

## Thursday 14 May

### AM 08:00-11:00 — Long Bike + Short Brick Run
**Purpose:** Long aerobic bike base. First brick run of the cycle. Practice race fueling.

**Bike — 2.5 to 3h**
- 0–90 min: Pure Z2, 130–165W, cadence 88–95 rpm. HR cap 148. This is the most important block. Do not drift above 148. If you do on a flat, you are going too hard. Ease off.
- 90–150 min: Upper Z2, 165–185W, cadence 90–95 rpm. HR allowed to rise to 155.
- 150–180 min: Low tempo, 185–205W, cadence 92+ rpm. HR 155–165. This is a controlled sustained effort — not a hard push.

**Fueling Protocol on Bike**
- Set a timer for every 20 min.
- Each alert: drink 150–200ml + take a gel or chew (20–30g carbs).
- Target: 70–90g carbs/hour, 600mg sodium/hour. Do not rely on hunger — you will be too late.
- This is race nutrition rehearsal as much as a training session.

**Brick Run — 20 min immediately off bike**
- Transition within 60 sec of stopping. Change shoes, go.
- HR will spike to 160–170 on first steps — this is normal and expected. Do not panic, do not slow to a walk. Start easy and let it settle.
- HR should return below 160 within 3 min. If it doesn't, slow slightly.
- Conversational effort throughout — this is neuromuscular training, not cardiovascular.
- Focus on running form not pace: tall posture, cadence 170+ spm, relaxed jaw and shoulders.

**KPI:** Cardiac drift on bike below 10 bpm over equal power blocks in the Z2 segment. Brick run HR settles within 3 min. Fueling alarm executed every 20 min — log it.

## Friday 15 May

### AM 08:30-10:00 — Long Run Z2
**Purpose:** The most important aerobic stimulus of the week. Do not compromise this.

**Session — 75–90 min, HR cap 150 strict**
- 0–15 min: Very easy, HR cap 140. Ease in, don't start at pace. Breathe, check posture, feel the feet.
- 15–70 min: Steady Z2, HR 140–150. Walk any hill that pushes HR above 150. This is not weakness — it is correct aerobic training. Pace on this segment will likely be 5:40–6:30/km depending on terrain and fatigue.
- Final 10 min: Wind down, HR below 135, jog fading to walk. Let HR drop naturally.

**Form Cues to Check Every 10 Min**
- Posture: tall, slight forward lean from ankles not waist
- Cadence: 170+ spm (use metronome app if needed — set to 174)
- Jaw: relaxed. If jaw is tight, upper body is tight.
- Shoulders: low, not hunched toward ears
- Glutes: actively driving each stride — not just quads pulling forward
- Breathing: nasal where possible. Switch to mouth only if needed. Keep it rhythmic.

**KPI:** No HR drift across the 15–70 min middle block. Final km pace within 15 sec of median pace. Perceived effort conversational throughout. Log avg HR, avg pace, and total time in Z2 band.

### AM 05:30-07:00 — Lower Strength + Sled Development
**Purpose:** Build lower body strength and Hyrox sled power without excessive fatigue.

**Warm-up 10 min**
- Hip circles x10 each direction
- Glute bridges 2x15 (2-sec hold at top)
- Goblet squat 2x10 light (pause 2 sec at bottom)
- Ankle rotations 10 each
- Banded clamshells 2x15 each side

**Back Squat — 4x5 @ RPE 7 (approx 80–87kg)**
- 3 min rest between sets
- Cues: brace 360 degrees (not just forward), knees track toes throughout, hit full depth (hip crease below knee), drive floor away on ascent. No forward torso collapse. If bar drifts forward, the weight is too heavy.
- Log: weight, RPE, any notes on depth or bar path.

**Romanian Deadlift — 4x6 @ RPE 7 (approx 75–85kg)**
- 2.5 min rest between sets
- Cues: push hips back not down, maintain neutral spine throughout, feel the hamstrings load at bottom before reversing, bar stays within 5cm of legs the entire way.
- Log: weight and RPE.

**Walking Lunge — 3x20m @ 40kg**
- 2 min rest between sets
- Cues: upright torso, rear knee hovers 2cm from floor, drive through front heel, do not let lead knee cave inward.

**Sled Push — 6x15m, moderate load, 60 sec rest**
- Lean forward at approximately 45 degrees from vertical. Short, powerful, fully-extended strides. Stay on balls of feet. Drive through each stride to full hip extension.

**Sled Pull — 6x15m, moderate load, 60 sec rest**
- Face away from sled. Drive arms back forcefully. Upright posture. Quick short steps. Stay leaning slightly back to keep tension on the rope.

**Core Finisher — 3 rounds, 45 sec rest between**
- Plank 60 sec: brace all three planes, don't let hips sag or pike
- Side plank 30 sec each side: keep hips stacked and high
- Dead bug 10 reps: lower back pinned to floor throughout

**KPI:** No form breakdown across any set. Squat depth consistent. Sled pace steady across sets. Log all working weights.

## Sunday 17 May

### AM 05:30-06:45 — Swim Technique + CSS Prep
**Purpose:** Reinforce technique from Thursday. Introduce more aerobic load.

**Warm-up**
- 300m easy mixed strokes. No effort. Focus on water feel and relaxed neck position.

**Drill Set — 8x50m (25m drill + 25m swim), 20 sec rest**
Cycle through drills:
- Catch-up drill: lead arm fully extended before opposite arm pulls
- Fingertip drag: drag fingertips along the water surface on recovery to enforce high elbow

**Aerobic Set — 6x100m, 20 sec rest**
- HR below 145 throughout. This should feel moderate and controlled.
- Target 16–18 stroke cycles per 25m length.
- 2-beat kick — not a powerful kick, just enough for balance.
- Exhale fully underwater before rotating to breathe. Head rotation should be minimal — one goggle in, one goggle out.

**Pull Set — 200m pull buoy, easy**
- Focus: press chest slightly down, high elbow catch, feel the forearm pressing back through the water (not just the palm).

**KPI:** Breathing pattern every 3 strokes throughout aerobic set. Relaxed neck and shoulders. Hips not sinking. Stroke count stable or decreasing across the aerobic set.

### PM 16:15-17:30 — Bike Threshold
**Purpose:** Second threshold stimulus of the week. Establish FTP repeatability.

**Warm-up 15 min**
- Progressive build from 100W to 170W
- Cadence 90–95 rpm
- Include 3x20 sec fast-pedal at 110+ rpm (at min 8, 10, 12) to prime legs

**Main Set — 3x10 min @ 218–230W (95–100% FTP), 5 min easy spin 120–140W between**
- Interval 1: Start at 218W. Let HR settle by min 2 (expect 168–172). Do not exceed 178.

**Cool-down 10 min**
- Easy spin from 160W down to 90W. Let legs spin freely.

**Fuel:** 40–60g fast carbs 20 min before starting. 500ml water during.

**KPI:** Power CV below 5% per interval. HR below 180 throughout. RPE 7–8 in final minute of each interval. Log avg power and avg HR per interval.
